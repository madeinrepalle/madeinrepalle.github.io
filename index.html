What Makes Us Special?

"Made in Repalle" is committed to supporting local farmers and showcasing the unique quality of our region's fruits.

Harvested at Peak Ripeness

We don't pluck early. Our fruits stay on the tree until they are perfectly ripe, ensuring maximum sweetness. Plucked today, sold today.

No harsh chemicals. We use time-tested, historic farming methods and natural fertilizers to nourish our soil and your family.
